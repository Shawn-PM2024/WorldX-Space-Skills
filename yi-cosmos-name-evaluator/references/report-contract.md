# Report contract

The final answer must lead with:

`名称｜整数分｜等级｜置信度｜敏感性区间`

Then preserve the generated Markdown sections:

1. 综合结论
2. 评分明细
3. 数据事实
4. 传统规则解释
5. 模型判断
6. 可选修正
7. 优势与风险
8. 建议
9. 证据缺口
10. 使用边界

## Required distinctions

- **数据事实:** input text, selected segment, Unicode variants, strokes, totals, and cited lexical or classical facts.
- **传统规则解释:** 81-number configuration, parity rule, trigram derivation, and optional calendrical interpretation.
- **模型判断:** semantic, industry, communication, five-element, and brand-fit conclusions.

Do not merge those categories or present a model judgment as sourced fact.

## Comparison mode

When comparing candidates, add one compact table with total score, confidence, sensitivity, the seven dimensions, and strongest risk. Use identical inputs, segmentation, weights, and evidence standards. A difference smaller than overlapping sensitivity intervals is not a decisive ranking.

## User-facing caveat

Always retain: `本结果属于可解释的传统文化命名模型，不构成经营、投资、法律或科学结论。`

If trademark, corporate registration, domain, linguistic taboo, or market validation is requested, perform it as a separate evidence stream and do not fold its result into the Yi-learning score without an explicitly revised model.
